Klipspringer Organizer Privacy Policy¶

Effective date: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are¶

This Privacy Policy explains how Axostichus LLC ("Klipspringer," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use Klipspringer.net and our services (the "Services").

Axostichus LLC
8735 Dunwoody Pl Ste R
Atlanta, GA 30350
United States

privacy@klipspringer.net

2. Two kinds of data, two roles¶

We handle two kinds of personal information, and our role is different for each.

Organizer data. This is information about the businesses that use the Services and the people who work with them. It includes account holders, team members, and billing contacts. For this data, we decide how and why it is used. In legal terms, we are the "controller" or "business." This Policy covers organizer data.

Attendee data. This is information about people who buy, register for, or receive tickets and other items from organizers. The organizer decides how and why this data is used. We process it only on the organizer's instructions, to provide the Services. 

If you are an attendee, the organizer's privacy policy applies to your data. Please send any requests to the organizer. If you send a request to us, we will forward it to the organizer.

3. What we collect about organizers¶

Information you give us:

  • Account information: your name, work email, organization name, and password. We store passwords only as cryptographic hashes.
  • Team information: the names and work emails of team members you invite.
  • Billing information: your billing contact, billing address, and tax or VAT number. If you pay an invoice by card, our payment provider collects your card details. We do not see or store full card numbers.
  • Communications: support requests, complaints, and emails you send us.

Information we collect automatically:

  • Usage and device data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and timestamps. We collect this through server logs and essential cookies.

Information from others:

  • Your Payment Processor: whether your account is connected, and the fees we deducted.
  • Single sign-on providers: your identifier and basic profile details, if you sign in that way.
  • Your colleagues: your name and work email, if a team member invites you.

We need your account and billing information to provide the Services. Without it, we cannot open or run your account.

4. How we use organizer data, and our legal bases¶

The law in the EU, UK, and Switzerland requires a legal basis for each use of personal information. The table lists ours.

What we do Our legal basis
Provide, operate, and support the Services Performing our contract with you
Bill you and administer your account Performing our contract with you. For tax and accounting records, complying with a legal obligation.
Send service messages, such as security alerts and changes to our Terms Performing our contract with you
Keep the Services secure and prevent fraud and abuse Our legitimate interest in protecting the Services, our users, and attendees
Review reports about content and handle complaints Complying with a legal obligation, and our legitimate interest in enforcing our Terms
Improve and develop the Services Our legitimate interest in making the Services better. Where we can, we use de-identified or aggregated data.
Comply with the law and respond to lawful requests Complying with a legal obligation. Where the obligation comes from a law outside the EU or UK, our legitimate interest in complying with it.
Handle a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets Our legitimate interest in running and transferring our business

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your rights. You may object at any time (see section 11).

5. Automated decisions¶

We may use automated tools to help detect fraud, abuse, and security threats. A person reviews the case before we suspend or terminate an account. We do not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you based only on automated processing.

6. How we share organizer data¶

We share organizer data only with:

  • service providers who help us run the Services, such as hosting and email delivery providers. Our contracts limit how they may use the data. Our current list is at docs.klipspringer.net/legal/subprocessors
  • your Payment Processor, to connect your account and collect our fees;
  • your team members, who can see the names and roles of others on your account;
  • professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, who owe us duties of confidence;
  • legal authorities, when the law requires it, or when we need to protect the rights, safety, or property of anyone; and
  • a successor entity, if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Your organization name and contact details appear on your public Event pages. You control what those pages show.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Cookies¶

We use only essential cookies. They keep you signed in and keep the Services secure. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Because these cookies are essential, the law does not require your consent for them.

8. How long we keep organizer data¶

  • Account and team information: while your account is open. We delete it within 90 days after your account closes.
  • Billing records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires, which is generally 7 years.
  • Support communications and complaints: for 3 years after your last contact with us.
  • Reports and moderation records: for 3 years after the decision, so we can handle complaints and show compliance.
  • Logs: security and audit logs for up to 12 months, with IP addresses shortened.
  • Backups: overwritten within 90 days.

We may keep data longer if the law requires it, or if we need it to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

9. Security¶

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. These include encryption in transit, access controls, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and hashed password storage. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Where we process organizer data¶

We are based in the United States, and we process organizer data there. If you are outside the United States, you give your information directly to us in the United States.

11. Your rights¶

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal information and get a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on consent; and
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

To exercise a right, email privacy@klipspringer.net. We may need to verify your identity first. In some US states, an authorized agent may make a request for you. We respond within one month. If a request is complex, we may extend that time as the law allows, and we will tell you if we do.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our response. We will answer your appeal within the time the law requires. If we deny your appeal, you may contact your state attorney general.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you may also complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office. In Switzerland, it is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

You may contact them instead of us about anything in this Policy.

12. Children¶

The Services are for businesses and organizations. We do not knowingly collect organizer data from anyone under 18.

13. Changes to this Policy¶

We may update this Policy. For material changes, we will tell you by email or by a message in the Services at least 30 days before the change takes effect. The effective date at the top shows when this Policy last changed.

14. Contact¶

Questions about this Policy can go to privacy@klipspringer.net or to our postal address in section 1.
