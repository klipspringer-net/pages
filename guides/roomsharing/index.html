Room sharing¶

Room sharing groups attendees who share lodging. Attendees can form groups with a code or you can match attendees who have no group. This may be useful if your event has shared lodging spaces (e.g., a camp or a group trip).

Rules¶

There are some rules you can set in regards to room sharing.

Rule Description
Minimum and maximum people The minimum and maximum group size. The maximum can be 2 to 12 people.
Approval Who can approve a new group.
Lock changes at The date and time after which attendees can't change their groups.
Same ticket tier Everyone in a group in a group is paired by ticket tier
Same gender Everyone in a group is paired by gender, which is declared using a custom question at checkout.

Managing groups¶

The Groups tab lists every group with its members and allows you to manage those groups. To download a CSV of all groups, choose "Export rooming list".
